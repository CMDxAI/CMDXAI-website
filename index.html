A platform that changes with you

CMDX is open source so organizations are not locked into a rigid learning environment. Host it where you want, change the workflows and interfaces, connect it to your other systems, and add what you need as training requirements change.

Developed in the open

Developers, trainers and organizations improve CMDX together on GitHub. Issues, discussions and pull requests are public.
