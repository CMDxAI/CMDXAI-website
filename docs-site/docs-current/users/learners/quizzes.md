Answer a quiz and review its result.

## Take the quiz

1. Open the course from **My Learning**.
2. Open the quiz item.
3. Read the instructions, time limit, and attempt information.
4. Select **Start quiz**.
5. Answer each question and save your progress as you go.
6. Review your answers, then select **Submit**.
7. Confirm the submission when asked.

Do not close the app during submission. A subjective answer may remain awaiting grading after you submit.

## Review the result

Open the quiz again or go to **My grades**. Depending on the course settings, you may see the score, feedback, or only a pending status.

## Troubleshooting

- **The quiz will not start:** check your enrollment and the availability dates.
- **The result is pending:** an instructor must manually grade one or more answers.
- **The attempt ended unexpectedly:** contact your instructor before starting another attempt.
