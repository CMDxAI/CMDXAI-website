Use CMDX on a phone or tablet.

## Sign in

1. Install the CMDX mobile app provided by your organization.
2. Open the app and enter the CMDX server address if requested.
3. Sign in with your CMDX account.
4. Open **My Learning** to see your courses.

The available screens and sign-in methods depend on the deployment and the mobile build.

## Learn on mobile

1. Open a course from **My Learning**.
2. Select an item to open the learner view.
3. Use the item navigation to move between sections and items.
4. Return to **My Learning** to check progress and continue later.

Keep the app updated and use a stable connection for uploads, SCORM packages, and media.

## Verify

Open a course, complete a small activity, and return to the course list. Confirm that progress is shown after the app synchronizes.

## Troubleshooting

- **The server address is rejected:** use the public CMDX address provided by your administrator.
- **A course is missing:** confirm that your account is enrolled and that you are signed in to the correct tenant.
- **Media or SCORM does not load:** switch networks and contact your administrator if the web app also fails.
