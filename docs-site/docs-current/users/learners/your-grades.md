Check scores and feedback for your course work.

## View your grades

1. Open **My grades** from the learner navigation.
2. Choose a course.
3. Review the item, score, maximum points, and status.
4. Open an item to read feedback when it is available.

A result can be **Awaiting grading** when an instructor must review the work. A score may also be hidden until the course releases it.

## Verify

Compare the grade with the submission or quiz attempt that produced it. If the result is pending, wait for the instructor to complete the review.

## Troubleshooting

- **A course is missing:** confirm that you are enrolled and using the correct tenant.
- **A score is pending:** the item needs manual grading.
- **Feedback is missing:** the instructor may not have released it yet.
