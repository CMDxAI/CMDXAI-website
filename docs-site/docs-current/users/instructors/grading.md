Review learner work and record grades.

## Before you start

You need grading permission for the course. The grading queue contains work that needs manual review; automatically scored work may already have a result.

## Grade work

1. Open the course and select **Grading**.
2. Filter the queue by course, item, or status when needed.
3. Open a learner's submission or quiz attempt.
4. Review the answers, uploaded files, or rubric entries.
5. Enter a score and feedback.
6. Select **Save grade** or the equivalent submit action.
7. Move to the next waiting submission.

A regrade keeps the previous grade in the grade history. Do not use a regrade to replace a submission unless the learner should be assessed again.

## Verify

Return to the grading queue and confirm that the item no longer appears as awaiting grading. Open the learner's grade view and check the score and feedback.

## Troubleshooting

- **The item is not in the queue:** it may be automatically graded, already graded, or assigned to another course.
- **The score is rejected:** check the maximum points and any rubric limits.
- **The learner cannot see the result:** check the result-release settings for the item or course.
