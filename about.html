About CMDX

Built by people who like open source and learning

CMDX exists to give organizations a modern learning platform they can run themselves.

The team has built and run learning platforms before and wanted one that organizations could deploy, change and extend without asking permission.

We want workplaces where learning is ongoing: people can see the skills their role needs, find the training, and get credit for it.

Open source keeps the platform flexible and affordable. AI, where it is useful, takes repetitive work off trainers.

Our vision

Enterprise-grade learning technology that any organization can run, because it is open source.

Our mission

Give organizations an open, flexible training platform they can keep improving themselves.

Open source learning, run on your terms.

An open source LMS for organizations, with AI built in.
